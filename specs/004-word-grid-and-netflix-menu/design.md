# 004 — Word Grid and a Netflix-style menu: Design

- **Status:** Implemented
- **Requirements:** [requirements.md](requirements.md)

## Word Grid (`public/games/wordgrid.js`)

- **Dictionary.** `public/content/dict.js` (generated by `npm run dict`) is one space-separated string. When the game is created it builds a `Set` of words plus a `Set` of every prefix, about 79k words and 200k prefixes, in under 100 ms on a TV.
- **Board generator.** Letters are drawn from English letter frequencies (`Q` becomes `Qu`), with at least 4 vowels on 4×4 and 6 on 5×5. A DFS solver walks all paths and prunes on the prefix set. Up to 40 candidate boards are tried, and the first that meets R2.1 is used. Otherwise the richest one is kept.
- **Helpers.** The solver and scoring are exposed as `window.WordGrid = { solve, points, makeBoard }` for unit tests.

### Protocol

| Direction | Message | Shape |
|---|---|---|
| TV → phone | `grid` | `{ ui:'grid', board:[16 or 25 strings], n, min, round, rounds, left (s), count, score }` |
| phone → TV | `word` | `{ t:'word', path:[tile indexes] }` |
| TV → phone | feedback | `{ wg:{ ok, word, pts, reason?, count, score } }`, which updates the open grid without re-rendering it |

The TV re-checks adjacency, reuse, length and the dictionary, so the phone is never trusted.

### Scoring

The points table is in R1.4. Base points go to `ctx.score` immediately, so the live strip moves. The unique bonus is added at round end.

### Rejoin

The runner re-sends the last `grid` with the current `left`, `count` and `score`. The phone's local list of found words resets, but the count and score come from the TV.

## Netflix-style menu (`tv.js`, `tv.css`)

- The games tab is a column: a **billboard** (fixed 44 vh) above a **rows area** (`overflow-y:auto`, scrollbar hidden).
- Each row's `.tiles` is `overflow-x:auto` with a hidden scrollbar and smooth scrolling.
- **Focusing a tile:**
  - `rowsArea.scrollTo({ top: row.offsetTop })` puts the focused row at the top of the rows area, as on Netflix;
  - `tiles.scrollTo({ left })` keeps the tile in view (tile left minus one tile of padding) when it would be clipped;
  - the billboard updates.
- Pointer users get ‹ › buttons on each row that scroll it by 80 % of its width. The wheel and touch scroll natively.
