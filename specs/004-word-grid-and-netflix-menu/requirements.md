# 004 — Word Grid and a Netflix-style menu: Requirements

- **Status:** Implemented (requested 2026-10-12)
- **Owner:** @Vimal27-20
- **Related:** [003-party-pack-v2](../003-party-pack-v2/requirements.md)

## Problem

1. The group wants a **Boggle-Party-style word game**, like the one on Netflix: a shared letter grid on the TV, with everyone tracing words on their phone at the same time.
2. On the TV, **not every game could be reached**. The Action row (6 games) is wider than the screen, it was clipped, and the rows couldn't be scrolled with a pointer remote or a mouse wheel.

## Decisions

- **Name.** The game is called **Word Grid**. "Boggle" is a Hasbro trademark, and this hub is published publicly, so we copy the *kind* of game, not the brand. It uses generated boards, not the Boggle dice set.
- **Dictionary.** SCOWL size ≤ 60: about 79k common English words under a permissive licence, with profanity removed. There are no obscure Scrabble-only words, so party players aren't punished for "real" words.

## Requirements

### R1 — Word Grid gameplay

1. THE SYSTEM SHALL show a 4×4 or 5×5 letter grid on the TV and the same grid on every phone. One tile is the two-letter **Qu**.
2. WHEN a player drags across adjacent tiles (including diagonals) and releases THE SYSTEM SHALL submit that word. Tapping tiles one by one and pressing Submit SHALL also work.
3. THE SYSTEM SHALL accept a word only if it follows adjacent unused tiles, is at least 3 letters (4 on 5×5), is in the dictionary, and that player hasn't already found it. Each phone SHALL get instant ✅ / ❌ / 🔁 feedback.
4. Points per word: 3–4 letters 1, 5 letters 2, 6 letters 3, 7 letters 5, 8+ letters 11. At the end of each round, a word found by only one player scores again (the **unique bonus**).
5. During a round the TV SHALL show each player's word count and points, and a live feed like "Ava found a 6-letter word!", without revealing the words.
6. WHEN a round ends THE SYSTEM SHALL show the longest word found, the unique bonuses, and the best words nobody found, before the round scoreboard.
7. Settings: rounds (2/3/5), round time (60/90/120 s) and board size (4×4 / 5×5).

### R2 — Always playable, always fresh

1. Every board SHALL be generated randomly and accepted only if it contains enough words: at least 60 on 4×4 or 150 on 5×5, with at least one word of 6+ letters. No two rounds share a board.
2. The dictionary SHALL be generated by a script from its source and committed, with the source's copyright notice kept.

### R3 — Netflix-style browsing on the TV

1. Every game SHALL be reachable with the remote: ↑ ↓ move between rows, and ← → move within a row. The focused row scrolls up under the billboard and the focused tile scrolls into view sideways.
2. Rows SHALL also scroll with a mouse wheel, a touchpad or a pointer remote (LG Magic Remote), with ‹ › arrows on each row.
3. The billboard (hero) SHALL always describe the focused game.

### R4 — Quality

1. Word Grid passes the bot playthrough (spec 003 R9.2). The board solver and scoring have unit tests.
2. The smoke test checks that the last tile of every row can be focused and is fully on screen.
